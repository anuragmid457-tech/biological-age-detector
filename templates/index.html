Your body keeps a different clock than your birthday.

Chronos estimates your biological age: how old your body is actually running, based on your lifestyle, history and blood test values. It also rates your overall health out of 100 and shows what's adding or taking away years.

What it looks at

Every question is optional. Answer what you know and skip the rest: the reading uses whatever you give it and says when it has little to go on.

Personal

Age, family longevity, sleep, height and weight.

Heart

Cholesterol, blood pressure, smoking, waist, stress and exercise.

Medical

Check-ups, diabetes, heart and lung conditions, medicines.

Blood tests

Glucose, HbA1c, CRP, kidney and liver values, blood count.

Nutrition

Meals, fruit and vegetables, fried and processed food, alcohol.

Psychological

Mood, anxiety, relationships, work and time with people.

Safety

Seat belts, helmets, distance travelled and risk-taking.

How a reading works

Two ways in, one reading out.

  1. Give your details

    Answer the questionnaire, or upload a PDF of a blood test or check-up.

  2. Get your reading

    Biological age, life expectancy, a health score out of 100, and the years each area added or removed.

  3. Ask about it

    Use the health chat to understand any value, and see which changes would help most.

Checked by clinicians

Doctors and medical experts can correct any reading and explain why it was wrong. Chronos keeps every correction and uses the most similar ones when it estimates the next person's age, so its judgement improves with each review.
